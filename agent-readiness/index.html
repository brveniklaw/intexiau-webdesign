The problem

Your site looks great to people. AI assistants may see something else.

Assistants read the raw page, not the design. An "Apply" button that runs on JavaScript, a phone number buried in an image, or one line in a settings file can stop them cold. When that happens, they send the person somewhere else, or make up an answer.

Before
I need help with an eviction notice in my county. Where do I apply?
I couldn't find an application link on their site. You might try searching for a local legal aid office.
After the fixes
They serve your county for free. You can apply online here, or call their intake line Monday to Friday, 9 to 4:30.
How it works

Audit, test, fix, and prove it.

You tell us the one thing you want visitors to do. We make sure every major assistant can get them there.

Audit

We score your key pages on about 30 checks: the path to your main action, structured data, readability and discoverability.

Live agent test

Re-test and report

We run the audit and agent tests again, ask the chat assistants your questions, and show you the before-and-after.

Questions

Good to know

Is this the same as SEO?

It builds on SEO but goes further. Search engines rank pages. AI assistants read your page and decide what to tell someone, often without them ever visiting. We make sure they get the facts right and point to your main action.

Will you redesign my site?

Only if you want us to. Most fixes are invisible to visitors: markup, structured data and settings files, kept in your current design. If your site needs more than fixes, our website plans start at $600.

Can I check my site myself first?

Yes. The free Agent Readiness checker gives you a score, your top issues and a live agent test. Add your email for the full list of issues. The exact fixes come with a Fix Report, or we make them for you and re-test.

What are my options?

Fix Report: every issue with the exact fix and ready-to-paste code, for you or your web person. Done for you: we make the fixes, re-test and show the before-and-after; a Fix Report bought in the last 30 days counts toward it. Monitoring: a monthly re-check, because assistants' answers change as their indexes update.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend us?

No one can, since each company decides how its assistant answers. What we can do is remove the barriers we find and show you, with a before-and-after test, how assistants handle your site.

Does this let AI companies train on my content?

That's your choice. We separate the crawlers that power answers from the ones that collect training data, so you can stay findable while opting out of training.

What do you need from us?

The page you care about most, the action you want visitors to take, and access to make changes to your site, or a contact on your web team.
