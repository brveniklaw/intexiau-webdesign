## Changed from the earlier kit

- **51% → 53%.** The kit said it was checked in November 2026 but used the 2025 report. Imperva's 2026 report (published April 2026) puts 2025 at 53%.
- **"US consumers" → "consumers"** for the 1 in 2 figure, matching McKinsey's own wording.
- **Dropped the 16% figure** ("brands that track AI search"). It wasn't used anywhere and wasn't rechecked.

## Don't use

- **"60%+ of traffic is AI agents."** No source supports this. The 53% figure covers all bots.
- **"51% of traffic is now automated."** Out of date; use 53% (2025).
- **Any 2027 traffic projection.** None was found. Use McKinsey's 2028 figure instead.
- **Gartner's "search volume down 25% by 2026."** The page couldn't be checked.
